`parse` and `print` support the hyperbolic functions `\sinh`, `\cosh`, `\tanh` and `\coth`, the common non-standard `\sech` and `\csch`, and the inverses `\arcsinh`, `\arccosh`, `\arctanh`, `\arccoth`, `\arcsech` and `\arccsch`. They used to throw `unknown command`.
